About this app

Dhunn is a beautiful, completely free music player for the songs already on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no tracking — your music never leaves your device, and everything plays fully offline.

What makes Dhunn different is how alive it feels: lyrics that follow the song with stunning live effects, 52 animated album art designs, HD covers fetched automatically, and themes that can take their colour from the cover of whatever is playing.

Synced lyrics, 240 effectsLyrics follow the song line by line, with live previews and your own custom colour. Fetch once — offline forever.
52 album art designsVinyl, CD, polaroid, glass, poster and more — every design animated, picked from a live gallery.
Equalizer & sleep timerFull equalizer with your own saved presets, bass boost and a sleep timer for the night.
HD covers, automaticallyMissing or low-quality album art is replaced with crisp HD covers — one tap can fix the whole library.
Themes & app iconsColour presets, AMOLED pure black, switchable app icons — or let the app match the playing cover.
Private by designNo account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your songs and your data stay on your phone.
