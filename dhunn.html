About this app

Dhunn is a beautiful, completely free music player for the songs already on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no tracking — your music never leaves your device, and everything plays fully offline.

What makes Dhunn different is how alive it feels: lyrics that follow the song with stunning live effects, 58 animated album art designs, HD covers fetched automatically, and a personal Music Report that knows your top songs and artists. YouTube Music search is built in too — songs play in the official YouTube player.

Synced lyrics, 40 live effectsLyrics follow the song line by line, with live previews and your own custom colour. Fetch once — offline forever.
58 album art designsVinyl, CD, polaroid, glass, poster and more — every design animated, picked from a live gallery.
Equalizer & sleep timerFull equalizer with your own saved presets, bass boost and a sleep timer for the night.
HD covers, automaticallyMissing or low-quality album art is replaced with crisp HD covers — one tap can fix the whole library.
Your Music ReportTotal plays, real listening time, your top songs and top artists — counted privately on your device.
Private by designNo account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your songs and your data stay on your phone.
